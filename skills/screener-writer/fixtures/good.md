# Screener: Work tools study (synthetic)

study: interview
platform: own panel
topic words: project management, Asana, Trello
intro: We are looking for people to take part in a 45-minute paid conversation about how they work. This short questionnaire takes about 3 minutes.

## Criteria
- C1: Works full time or part time
- C2: Has used a project management tool at work in the past month
- C3: Chooses or recommends software for their team
- C4: Does not work in market research, UX research or advertising (exclude)

## Quotas
total: 12
- Company size (Q4): "1 to 49 employees" 4-6; "50 to 999 employees" 4-6; "1,000 or more employees" 2-4

## Questions

### Q1 [single] (C1) randomize
Which of these best describes your current employment?
- Employed full time -> continue
- Employed part time -> continue
- Not currently employed -> terminate
- Retired -> terminate
- Full-time student -> terminate
- Prefer not to say -> terminate

### Q2 [single] (C4) randomize
Which industry do you mainly work in?
- Healthcare -> continue
- Retail or hospitality -> continue
- Software or technology -> continue
- Education -> continue
- Market research or UX research -> terminate
- Advertising or a marketing agency -> terminate
- Another industry -> continue

### Q3 [multi] (C2) randomize
Which of these have you used at work in the past month? Select all that apply.
- Google Sheets -> continue
- Asana -> qualify
- Trello -> qualify
- Zoom -> continue
- Expensify -> continue
- Confluence -> continue
- None of these -> terminate

### Q4 [single]
How many people work at your organization, across all locations?
- 1 to 49 employees -> continue
- 50 to 999 employees -> continue
- 1,000 or more employees -> continue
- Don't know -> terminate

### Q5 [single] (C3) randomize
When your team adds a new piece of software, what is your role in that decision?
- I make the final decision -> qualify
- I recommend options to the person who decides -> qualify
- I am asked for feedback after it is chosen -> terminate
- I am not involved -> terminate
- The decision is made by another department -> terminate

### Q6 [open] articulation
Tell us about the last time you had to keep a group project on track at work. What did you do?
