# Screener: NN/g examples (Whitenton 2019)

study: interview
topic words: scooter, scooters, online games

## Criteria
- C1: Plays online games
- C2: Has ridden a shared scooter recently

## Questions

### Q1 [single] (C1)
Do you play online games?
- Yes -> qualify
- No -> terminate

### Q2 [multi] (C2) randomize
Which of these ways of getting around have you used in the past month? Select all that apply.
- Walking more than a mile -> continue
- A ride-sharing app -> continue
- A taxi -> continue
- A shared electric scooter -> qualify
- None of these -> terminate

### Q3 [open] articulation
What activities do you usually do online in a typical week?
